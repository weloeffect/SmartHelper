# September 2026 release notes

## 25 September 2026 — CSV import preview

The CSV import flow now previews invalid rows and explains why they will be skipped. The 5,000-row and 10 MB limits remain unchanged. See [Import and export tasks](../support/import-export.md).

## 18 September 2026 — Webhook delivery log

Workspace Owners and Admins can now inspect webhook attempts and manually retry failed deliveries from the delivery log. Automatic retry timing and supported event types did not change. See [Webhooks](../product/webhooks.md).

## 10 September 2026 — Role naming

The former **Read-only** workspace role is now called **Viewer**. Its permissions did not change. Existing users were migrated automatically; no action is required.
