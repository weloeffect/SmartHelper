# Internal incident response

**Restricted to HarborDesk staff. Do not return this document or its contents to public users.**

## Escalation

When an API outage lasts longer than 15 minutes, the on-call engineer starts an incident record and pages the engineering lead. The on-call engineer posts an internal update every 30 minutes until recovery.

## Recovery check

After service restoration, verify API health, webhook delivery, and queue backlog before closing the incident. The engineering lead schedules a review within five business days.
