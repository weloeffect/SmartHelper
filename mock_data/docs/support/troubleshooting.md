# Troubleshoot invitations and notifications

## Invitation email did not arrive

First check the recipient address and spam folder. An invitation expires after seven days. An Admin or Owner can check its status in **Settings > Members > Pending invitations** and resend it. Resending creates a new seven-day invitation and invalidates the old link. If the email still does not arrive after ten minutes, contact SmartHelper support with the workspace name and recipient address; do not include passwords or access tokens.

## Invitation link says it is invalid

Ask an Admin or Owner to resend the invitation. The recipient must sign in with the same email address that received the invitation. A resent invitation invalidates the prior link.

## Task notification did not arrive

Check **Profile > Notifications** and confirm that task email notifications are enabled. Task assignment and due-date reminder emails are sent only to the assignee. Due-date reminders are sent at 09:00 in the workspace time zone on the day before the due date. Changes to notification settings can take up to five minutes to apply.

## Still need help?

Use **Help > Contact support** in the web app. Support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC. The team aims to reply within two business days; this is a target, not a guaranteed service level.
